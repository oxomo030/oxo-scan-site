Privacy Policy

oxo-scan for iPhone and iPad · Last updated: 17 November 2026

In short

oxo-scan does not collect any data. Your scans never reach us — we have no server, no account and no way to see them.

What happens on your device

  • The camera is used only to scan paper documents while the scanner is open.
  • Finding the page, straightening it, improving the image and creating the PDF all happen on your iPhone or iPad.
  • Photos are not saved to your photo library.

Where your scans are stored

  • In your own iCloud Drive (folder “oxo-scan”) when you are signed in to iCloud, so they appear on your other devices. This is your personal storage with Apple; we cannot access it. Apple’s privacy policy applies to iCloud.
  • Otherwise on the device itself (Files › On My iPhone › oxo-scan), or in a folder you choose.
  • Deleting a scan in the app or in the Files app removes it. Deleting the app removes the scans stored on the device; scans in iCloud Drive stay until you delete them.

What we do not do

  • No account, no sign-in.
  • No analytics, no crash reporting to us, no advertising, no tracking.
  • No cookies or tracking on this website.
  • No data is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

Sharing

A PDF leaves your device only when you share it yourself (for example by e-mail or AirDrop). What happens to it then is up to you and the app you share it with.

Settings

The app remembers your choices (for example the storage folder) on the device only.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.

Contact
