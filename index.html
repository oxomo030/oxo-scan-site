oxo-scan

Scan paper documents to PDF — on your iPhone and iPad.

oxo-scan finds the page, takes the picture when it is sharp, straightens it and saves a clean PDF. Everything happens on your device: no account, no server, no subscription.

Help

The camera stays black

oxo-scan needs access to the camera. Open Settings › Apps › oxo-scan and turn on Camera.

Where are my scans?

  • Signed in to iCloud: in the Files app under iCloud Drive › oxo-scan — also on your other devices and on the Mac.
  • Without iCloud: in the Files app under On My iPhone › oxo-scan (or On My iPad).
  • You can choose a different folder in the app under Settings › Storage.

The page is not captured automatically

Put the page on a surface with some contrast, hold the device steady above it and make sure there is enough light. You can always use the shutter button.

The page looks blurry

Move the device a little further away, or turn on Macro for small pages such as receipts. You can retake any page before saving.
